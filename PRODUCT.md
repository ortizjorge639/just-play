# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Gamers choosing what to play and remembering the games they finish.

## Product Purpose

Just Play tracks games through Backlog, In Progress, and Completed. Each completed
game becomes a character living in the user's treehouse.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The production app uses Next.js 16, React 19, TypeScript, Supabase, IGDB,
Tailwind v4, shadcn/ui, and Framer Motion.

The approved first deliverable is a self-contained HTML prototype with Three.js
WebGPURenderer, original procedural assets, labeled sample data, and local
persistence. Production integration and any shared-branch push or merge require
separate approval. The prototype must not use production credentials or data.

## Brand Commitments

Toy-like, diorama-scale, and very cozy. Animal Crossing: New Horizons informs the
materials, pacing, and approachable interaction, not copied assets or characters.

## Product Principles

- Getting back to a game comes before collecting rewards.
- A completed game becomes a tangible memory, not another statistic.
- Sessions count without pressure to maintain a streak.
- Core tracking remains usable without 3D or animation.
